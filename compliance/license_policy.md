# Third-Party License Policy

Before a model or dataset may be used in a production release it must be
classified as one of:

- **approved** - permissive license that allows commercial use without further legal review
- **restricted** - non-commercial, share-alike, or custom terms that require legal review
- **blocked** - missing, unknown, or unresolved license terms; usage must not proceed

Restricted and blocked assets require a follow-up review issue. Approved assets
do not.

License values must be read from the upstream repository card on Hugging Face,
not from internal copies.
